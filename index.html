    03 — Identity System

    Identity

    KaRAKuLE carries a dual meaning: the dark stone watchtower of ancient cities, and a Turkish idiom for a tall, dark-complexioned, commanding presence. It stands high, sees far, and sends a signal when it matters.

    Mark — Watchtower + K

    A three-battlement watchtower with a carved K in its monolith. The orange square at the crown is the sentinel beacon: the tower is awake.

    04 — How I Work

    Process

    S/01 · ~1 week

    Observe

    Listening to the brand, sector, and audience. Looking at what is actually done rather than what is merely claimed.

    Output: Research brief, direction map
    S/02 · ~1–2 weeks

    Structure

    Distilling the core idea into one sentence, then turning that sentence into a system: grid, color, type, motion.

    Output: Concept, system architecture
    S/03 · ~2–6 weeks

    Build

    Design, prototype, and code on the same table. Every week you see something working, not filler slides.

    Output: Design system, visuals, code
    S/04 · Ongoing

    Launch

    Deployment, refinement, and handover. Guidelines and source files delivered so your brand stands on its own.

    Output: Brand guide, source assets
